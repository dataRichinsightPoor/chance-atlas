DIAGNOSTICS

Five measurements that separate the mechanisms.

Most entries in the catalog are exposed by one of a small set of measurements, and the measurements are cheap compared with the experiments they protect.

Variance against mean. Poisson processes have variance equal to the mean; a Fano factor above one measures burst size, aggregation or amplification noise; a parabola in the mean reveals binomial gating. Plotting variance against mean across a dilution series or a time course is the first diagnostic for almost every entry in sections A through D.

The zero class. Empty wells, cultures with no mutants, cells with no detected transcript and droplets with no signal each estimate a rate through e−λ, and the estimate is robust to everything that happens after the zero is set. The Luria–Delbrück p0 method, the empty-well calibration of limiting dilution and the digital PCR count share this logic.

Dual reporters and siblings. Two identical reporters in one cell separate intrinsic from extrinsic noise; sister cells separate inherited state from fresh fluctuation; split-input technical replicates separate amplification noise from biology. Each design asks the same question: how much of the variance is shared by two measurements that should agree?

The null distribution from controls. Non-targeting guides, mock-sorted populations, unlabeled secondaries and directly labeled primaries give the distribution of outcomes when the mechanism of interest is absent. A hit is a departure from that distribution, not from zero.

Variance components. Controls replicated across wells, plates and days give the three variances that decide where to spend replicates and how wide the real interval is. A standard error computed from wells alone is a statement about pipetting, not about the condition.

BOUNDARIES

What this atlas is and is not.

Closed forms, not simulations of your experiment. Each calculator evaluates an idealized model with the parameters you enter. The models are standard and tested against simulation, but the parameters are yours to justify, and none is estimated from data here.
Catalog, not census. The processes listed are the well-documented ones with primary references. Other sources of randomness exist, and apparent stochasticity (section F) is a reminder that some of what looks random is a covariate that was not recorded.
No experimental data. Every number on this page is synthetic forward-model output. The implementation is original code written from the public literature cited in each entry. No employer-provided data, constructs, methods or internal results were used.
